Run `node server/migrations/run-migration53.js` before deploying inspection stock updates.

The additive migration reuses the existing unique refund/item restock ledger. It does not restock historical returns. Inspection saves require physical receipt, notes, a claim decision, and a returned/accepted quantity and resale condition for every requested item. Stock uses the original order item's product ID (this repository has no separate variant stock table). Inspection results become immutable after saving; repeated identical saves do not add stock or audit events. Refund approval and rejection can also save an inspection atomically.

Selecting Suitable for resale = Yes opens a photo and confirmation dialog. The admin must confirm every listed requested quantity was physically received and inspected before restocking. Partial-return quantities remain supported by the service; the simplified form's Yes action confirms the complete listed return. No hides the stock controls and records zero resale-accepted quantity when the refund decision is saved. Resale photo proof uses the existing admin-only private `inspection` file kind; confirmation, proof, stock movements and inspection results commit together. Previously saved inspections retain their original evidence requirements.
